# Code of conduct

## Our commitment

Folio welcomes people of every background, identity, ability, and experience level. Everyone should be able to participate without harassment.

## Expected behavior

Be considerate, discuss ideas respectfully, welcome questions, acknowledge mistakes, and accept constructive feedback. Respect privacy, accessibility needs, and other people's time.

Harassment, discrimination, threats, sexualized attention, personal attacks, doxxing, and publishing confidential documents are unacceptable.

## Scope and reporting

This policy covers repository issues, pull requests, reviews, and interactions representing the project.

Use GitHub's **Report content** or **Report abuse** tools on the relevant content or profile to report conduct concerns privately to GitHub. See [GitHub's reporting guidance](https://docs.github.com/en/communities/maintaining-your-safety-on-github/reporting-abuse-or-spam). No private maintainer email is currently published. Do not post sensitive incident details in a public issue.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may remove harmful content and restrict participation. Depending on severity and repetition, responses include a correction, warning, temporary restriction, or permanent ban. Reports should be handled fairly; personal information must remain confidential. Retaliation is prohibited.

This project-specific policy is informed by the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).
